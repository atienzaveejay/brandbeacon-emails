Our version, same set

The same eight flows and the same 20 emails as BrandBeacon product email (Ivan plain text) of 9 September, matched one for one so the two boards read side by side. Same triggers, same cadences, same merge tags. Our copy. Flow 3 is locked in Ivan's doc, so all four of those are his words unchanged and are identical on both boards.

An email here is type. It may add a picture and a button. Nothing else. No filled brand bars, no stat bands, no cards, no score badges, no timelines. Anything those elements used to carry now lives in the sentence, which is where it belongs. Button labels are actions, never sentences: the longest on this page is four words.

Two emails sit past the twenty and are marked as such: a card expiring notice, and a quarterly send for closed accounts. Neither is in Ivan's program. Both are proposals, kept separate so the comparison stays clean. Grey lines under each title say what is different from Ivan's version of the same email, and why.

Ivan's copy, designed

All 20 emails across the eight flows, exactly as written in BrandBeacon product email (Ivan plain text) of 9 September. No sentence has been rewritten, reordered, trimmed or added to. What changed is only how each email is set: the numbers already inside Ivan’s sentences are pulled up so they can be scanned, lists become lists, and the one instruction in each email becomes the button. Merge tags stay visible as chips so it is obvious what is dynamic.

Two liberties, both typographic, both worth naming. Where Ivan wrote a clause ending in a link, such as You can run it here: {{app_url}}, the clause becomes the button and the URL sits under it, so the trailing colon goes. And the small labels on the stat bands are ours, not his: every number inside them is either Ivan’s own figure or a merge tag, and nothing has been invented. Grey lines under each title say what the design is doing and why.
